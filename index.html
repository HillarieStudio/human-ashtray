Our Purpose

A Life, Held

At Human Ashtray, we believe a life well lived should not be placed on a shelf and forgotten. It should be held. It should be used. It should remain part of the everyday rituals our loved ones cherished most.

Each person entrusted to our care is honored in a limited series of forty handcrafted memorial ashtrays. Their cremated remains are gently folded into a custom clay body, and every tray is shaped, pressed, glazed and kiln-fired by hand. At the center of each one rests an impression of their face, so that every time a cigarette is set down, it is set down with them.

Some of the people in our collection chose Human Ashtray for themselves, and made their final wishes known to family before they passed. Others were lovingly remembered by friends and family who donated them in their honor. Every series is individually numbered and limited to forty. When the forty are gone, they are gone.

Gathered

Remains are received with dignity and blended into our clay by hand, never by machine.

Pressed

A likeness of the face is taken from photographs and pressed into the center of each tray.

Fired

Each of the forty is kiln-fired, glazed, numbered and sent home to be loved.

A portion of every series supports the work of the American Lung Remembrance Society.

Where It Began

A Letter From Our Family

Our Gram, Ethell, was never without two things: a cigarette, and somewhere lovely to set it down. She kept ashtrays the way other grandmothers keep teacups. Crystal ones, ceramic ones, a seashell from Myrtle Beach, a little brass one shaped like Florida. She dusted every one of them on Sundays.

When we would lovingly tell Gram she ought to think about quitting, for her health, she would lovingly holler back from her recliner, “Let me die and turn me into a human ashtray!”

So we did.

Today all forty of Gram’s ashtrays are out in the world, being loved as much as she was. Human Ashtray began with her, and every series we make carries a little of her with it.

With love,

A&H
