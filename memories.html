A Note Before You Write

We have to be honest with you.

Human Ashtray isn’t real. Nobody has been turned into an ashtray. Ethell, Ron, Darlene and the rest never lived, no remains were ever mixed into any clay, and there is no American Lung Remembrance Society (though maybe there should be).

It’s a joke, a dark one, made by two people who think death is a little less scary when you can laugh at it.

If you came here because you’re missing someone for real, we’re sorry, and we hope Gram made you laugh anyway.

Now that you’re in on it, we’d love to hear from you. Leave a review, a memory, or your idea for a future Human Ashtray. The best ones may end up on the site.
